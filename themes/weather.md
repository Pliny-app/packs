# Weather

## Sky
- **Clear:** cloudless, bright, crisp, open, washed blue
- **Cloudy:** overcast, leaden, grey, heavy, low, brooding, mackerel sky
- **Clouds:** cumulus (heaped, cotton), stratus (flat sheet), cirrus (high wisps), cumulonimbus (thunderhead, anvil)
- **Light:** glare, haze, gloaming, dusk, half-light, sun-shower, break in the clouds

## Rain
- **Light:** drizzle, mizzle, spitting, sprinkle, fine rain, mist
- **Heavy:** downpour, deluge, cloudburst, torrent, sheets of rain, lashing, teeming
- **Passing:** shower, squall, flurry, spell of rain
- **After:** puddles, runoff, dripping eaves, petrichor (the smell of rain on dry ground)

## Wind
- **Gentle:** breeze, breath of air, zephyr, light airs
- **Strong:** gust, gale, squall, blast, howling, buffeting
- **Named:** sirocco (hot, from the Sahara), mistral (cold, southern France), chinook (warm, dry, down the Rockies), monsoon (seasonal, wet)

## Storms
- thunderstorm, tempest, hurricane, typhoon, cyclone, tornado, twister
- thunder (rumble, crack, peal, clap), lightning (fork, sheet, bolt, flash)

## Cold
- **Frost:** hoarfrost, rime, black ice, glaze, frozen fog
- **Snow:** flurries, sleet, slush, powder, drift, blizzard, whiteout
- **Feel:** biting, bitter, raw, nippy, numbing, bone-chilling

## Heat
- **Dry:** scorching, baking, parched, blistering, searing
- **Wet:** humid, muggy, close, sticky, sultry, oppressive
- **Spells:** heatwave, dog days, Indian summer (warm weather in autumn)
