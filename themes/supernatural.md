# Supernatural

## The other side
- **realm** — a world or domain of its own: the spirit realm, the realm of the dead.
- **the veil** — the thin boundary between the living world and what lies past it. To **pierce the veil** is to see or cross through.
- **the beyond · the otherworld · the hereafter** — the place past death or past ordinary life; the second has a fairy-tale feel, the third a religious one.
- **dimension · plane** — a separate layer of reality; *plane* is the occult word, as in the **astral plane**.
- **portal** — a doorway between worlds.
- **underworld · netherworld** — the realm below, of the dead.
- **limbo** — a state between, neither one place nor the other.
- **parallel world** — a reality running alongside ours.

## Spirits and presences
- **spirit** — the general word for a being without a body.
- **ghost** — the spirit of someone dead, still here.
- **specter** (UK *spectre*) — a ghost that frightens; also a looming threat: the specter of war.
- **phantom** — a ghost, or anything that seems real but isn't: a phantom sound.
- **apparition** — a ghostly figure seen briefly.
- **shade** — a ghost of the classical underworld; quiet and faded.
- **wraith** — a thin, pale ghost; in Scottish lore, the double of a living person, seen before their death.
- **revenant** — someone who has come back from the dead, often for a reason.
- **poltergeist** — a "noisy spirit" that throws things and knocks on walls.
- **banshee** — in Irish lore, a woman whose wailing foretells a death.
- **will-o'-the-wisp** — a ghostly light over marshes that leads travelers astray.
- **familiar** — a spirit in animal form that serves a witch.
- **guardian** — a protecting spirit, bound to a person or place.
- **entity · presence** — something felt or sensed, its nature unknown.
- **demon** — a malevolent spirit.
- **celestials** — heavenly beings.

## Haunting
- **haunt** — to return again and again to a place or person.
- **linger · dwell** — to stay on where one no longer belongs.
- **manifest · materialize** — to become visible or physical; *materialize* stresses taking solid form.
- **appear · vanish · disappear** — to come and go; *vanish* is sudden and complete.
- **fade · dissipate** — to thin away slowly, like mist.
- **glide · drift** — to move without walking; ghosts don't take steps.
- **flicker** — to come and go like a failing light.
- **possess** — to take over a body or an object from within.
- **murmur · whisper · moan · wail** — the voices of the unseen, from soft to anguished.
- **echo** — a sound, or an event, that repeats after it's gone.

## Spellwork
- **magic** — the general word. **Magick**, Aleister Crowley's spelling, marks ritual practice rather than stage tricks.
- **sorcery** — magic worked through spirits or dark powers; it has a sinister edge.
- **witchcraft** — the craft of witches; also a living religious practice for some.
- **wizardry** — learned, bookish magic; also, any dazzling skill.
- **spell** — a magical act or its words.
- **incantation** — the words of a spell, spoken or sung.
- **chant** — to recite rhythmically, again and again.
- **charm** — a small spell, often protective; also an object that carries one.
- **hex** — a spell meant to harm; folk and practical.
- **curse** — a lasting harm laid on someone; heavier than a hex.
- **jinx** — bad luck that follows someone; lighter than a hex.
- **enchant** — to put under a spell; also to delight.
- **bewitch · beguile** — to charm into helplessness; to charm by trickery.
- **conjure** — to make appear by magic.
- **summon** — to call a being to come.
- **invoke · evoke** — in ritual magic, to *invoke* is to call a power into yourself or upon you, and to *evoke* is to call a spirit to appear outside you.
- **banish · dispel** — to send a spirit away; to break an illusion or spell.
- **bind** — to hold a spirit or power under control.
- **ward** — a protective barrier, or the act of setting one.
- **transmute** — to change one substance into another, as alchemists hoped to make gold.
- **illusion · glamour** — a false appearance; *glamour* is the old word for a spell of seeming.
- **ritual · rite · ceremony** — set acts performed in order; *rite* is the single act, *ceremony* the occasion.

## Tools of the craft
- **spellbook · grimoire** — a book of spells; *grimoire* is older and darker.
- **altar** — the table or stone where offerings and rituals are made.
- **circle** — a ring drawn for protection or power.
- **sigil** — a symbol made for one specific purpose or spirit.
- **rune** — a letter of the old Germanic alphabets, used in carving and divination.
- **pentagram · pentacle** — a five-pointed star; a *pentacle* is one set in a circle, or a disc carrying it. A sacred symbol in Wicca and modern paganism.
- **elixir · potion · philter** — a magic drink: one that heals or preserves life; one for any purpose; one that makes someone fall in love.
- **candle · incense · salt** — common ritual materials; salt is a traditional protection.
- **amulet** — an object worn to protect against harm.
- **talisman** — an object that brings power or luck. (Amulets defend; talismans attract.)
- **moonstone · crystal** — stones prized as charms and focuses.
- **relic** — an object left by someone holy or long dead and said to hold power.
- **spirit board / talking board** — a lettered board used to speak with the dead. *Ouija* is a trademark.
- **tarot** — a deck of picture cards used for readings.

## Seers and seeing
- **seer** — one who sees what others can't, often the future.
- **oracle** — a person or place through which a god speaks; also the message itself.
- **prophet** — one who speaks for a higher power, often warning.
- **medium** — one who speaks with the dead.
- **psychic · clairvoyant** — one with mental powers; specifically, one who sees what's hidden.
- **mystic** — one who seeks direct union with the divine.
- **augur · diviner** — readers of signs: the first, of birds in old Rome; the second, of anything.
- **channel** — to let a spirit speak or act through you.
- **trance** — a half-awake state in which visions come.
- **séance** — a gathering to contact the dead.
- **divination · scrying** — reading the hidden; gazing into water, mirrors or crystal for visions.
- **vision** — something seen in a dream or trance.
- **prophecy** (noun) / **prophesy** (verb) — a foretelling; to foretell. *Prophecy* rhymes with "see," *prophesy* with "sigh."
- **omen · portent · sign** — a hint of what's to come; *portent* is weightier, often grim.
- **aura** — a field of light or feeling said to surround a person.

## Change and power
- **shape-shift · transform** — to change form; to change in any way.
- **levitate** — to float off the ground.
- **transcend · ascend** — to rise past the ordinary; to rise to a higher state.
- **awaken** — to rouse, or be roused, to power or awareness.
- **resurrect · raise** — to bring the dead back to life; *raise* is the necromancer's word.
- **radiate · illuminate** — to give off light or power; to light up, or make understood.
- **shimmer** — to tremble with light, at the edge of seeing.
- **shift** — to slip between forms or worlds.

## Describing the uncanny
- **supernatural** — beyond the laws of nature.
- **preternatural** — beyond the normal, but not necessarily magical: preternaturally calm.
- **paranormal** — beyond what science can explain; the word of ghost hunters.
- **otherworldly · unearthly** — not of this world; the second, often eerily so.
- **ethereal** — so light and fine it barely seems real.
- **spectral · phantasmal · ghostly** — like a ghost; like an illusion; the plain word.
- **translucent** — letting light through, but not clearly.
- **shadowy · shrouded · veiled · eclipsed** — dim; wrapped and hidden; half-covered; blotted out.
- **unseen** — present but invisible.
- **arcane · esoteric · occult** — known to few; meant for the initiated; hidden.
- **cryptic · enigmatic** — puzzling on purpose; mysterious in nature. An **enigma** is the mystery itself.
- **mystical** — of hidden spiritual truth.
- **eldritch** — weird and unsettling, in Lovecraft's vein.
- **unfathomable** — too deep to understand.
- **foreboding · ominous · baleful** — threatening what's to come; menacing; full of harm.
- **bewitched · enchanted · cursed · hexed · haunted** — under a spell; charmed or beautiful; harmed by magic; harmed by a hex; inhabited by spirits.
- **unholy · profane** — against what is sacred.
- **sacred · hallowed · divine** — holy; made holy; of a god.
- **celestial · astral** — of the heavens; of the stars, or of the spirit plane.
- **infernal · chthonic** — of hell; of the underworld and earth below.
- **fey** — touched by fairy; also, oddly otherworldly.
- **ancient · eternal · immortal · omnipresent** — very old; without end; unable to die; everywhere at once.

## Hours and seasons
- **twilight · gloaming · dusk** — the half-light after sunset; *gloaming* is Scottish and soft.
- **witching hour** — midnight, or the dead hours after it, when spirits are said to be strongest.
- **full moon · new moon · blood moon** — bright; dark; red during an eclipse.
- **eclipse** — the sun or moon blotted out; long read as an omen.
- **solstice · equinox** — the longest and shortest days; the days of equal light and dark.
- **Samhain** — the Gaelic festival at the end of October, when the veil is said to be thinnest; roots of Halloween.
- **All Hallows' Eve** — the night before All Saints' Day; Halloween.
